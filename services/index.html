What we do

Seven services, one standard

We are a design–build studio. The same crew that draws the plan sets the stone and plants the beds, seven ways.

01

Design

Measured site plans and planting drawings before anything is built.
02

Build

One crew from first cut to final sweep — no subcontracted hand-offs.
03

Stonework

Dry-laid walls, cut-stone steps and flagstone terraces set to last.
04

Plantings

Beds chosen for the site’s light, soil and water — not a catalogue.
05

Outdoor living

Kitchens, fire features and shade structures matched to the house.
06

Lighting

Low-voltage paths, uplit specimens and warm 2700K fixtures.
07

Drainage

Grading, dry creeks and French drains that move water away quietly.
